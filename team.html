Principal Investigator

SJK

Sheima J. Khatib, Ph.D.

Associate Professor · Department of Chemical Engineering · Virginia Tech

Dr. Khatib leads the Khatib Laboratory, performing research on diverse aspects of heterogeneous catalysis at the atomic and nanoscale level. Her work spans catalyst synthesis, structural characterization, kinetic testing, and catalytic reactor engineering, all applied to chemically sustainable processes with industrial and energy applications. Her mission is to prepare novel optimized catalysts for efficient chemical conversion that can answer technological demands and societal challenges.
